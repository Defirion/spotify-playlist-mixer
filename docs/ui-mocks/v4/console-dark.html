Mix playlists by ratio.

    Saved playlists stay off your profile, but anyone with the link can open them.

    Design notes
    Palette
    Page #131517, chassis #25292C, strips #2E3236, wells #181A1C, silkscreen text #E6E8EA and #A4ABB1, one orange #FF7A1A for the primary action. Data colours: #E4572E, #2BB5A4, #E0A100, #7FA83A, #C9588A, #6C93BF.
    Type
    IBM Plex Sans for words, IBM Plex Mono for numbers. 13px minimum.
    Ideas
    Hardware cues carry the meaning so the words can go: knobs for songs in a row, a scaled fader for priority, an LED meter for share. Blend and Review are separate steps. Exact values and the example order sit behind Fine tune.
    Dark only
    No white surface anywhere, including the page background before load, dialogs and inputs. The brightest areas are the small knob caps and the active toggle.
    Trade-off
    Less text means the knobs and the meter must be learned once. Names and aria labels carry the full wording for assistive tech.
