    Design notes — E · Aurora

    Palette

    #060814 night#0A84FF accent #FF4F9A bachata#FFA43A salsa #2FD9C5 kizomba#FFB340 warning glass white 12–17%

    Fonts

    Figtree for UI and headings. Manrope with tabular numerals for every stat, weight and duration, so digits never jitter while you drag.

    Signature ideas

    • The background is the data. Each selected playlist is a colour blob whose size follows its priority weight, so the field is a live picture of the mix ratio.
    • Blend bar. One draggable band per source with typed weights beside it; handles trade weight between neighbours.
    • Glass only where things float. Toolbar, cards, action bar and the lifted row get blur; rows stay flat. Dragging lifts a row into glass with a glow.

    Trade-off

    Drama over density. Blur plus a moving colour field costs GPU on low-end devices and legibility depends on the field staying mid-dark, so text is always white with a tinted glass floor. You see fewer rows per screen than a flat list would give.
