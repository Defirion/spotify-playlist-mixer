Round 2: six directions

Reworked against a list of common AI-design tells: purple gradients, Inter everywhere, gradient headlines, intro pills, glow and frosted glass, icons in rounded squares, identical card rows, coloured side stripes, hero stats, tiny labels, fake status dots, marketing copy with em dashes, and the beige-plus-italic-serif escape. Same app, same data, same features. Each file is self-contained; use its "mock controls" bar to flip Connect and Workspace and trigger toasts.

A · studio gear

Console

A plain analogue mixer: one channel per playlist with a real fader, two knobs for songs in a row, and a live meter. Labels are plain words, and the totals are one sentence.

Trade-off: Most intuitive ratio controls, but tall.

B · pro tool, daylight

Studio

Light, dense working tool. A draggable share bar, a table of numeric fields, and a timeline with one lane per playlist that shows the generated sequence. Summary bar at the bottom.

Trade-off: Clearest picture of what the mix does, but a power-user screen.

C · radio running order

Rundown

A producer's running-order sheet: white paper, ruled rows, mono start times that recompute as you drag tracks. A highlighter marks only the current selection. Replaces the beige-and-serif Liner Notes.

Trade-off: Very legible and fast to scan, plain by design.

D · two-ink riso print

Mixtape

One flat illustrated cassette whose tape window is the live ratio meter. Two inks and overprint on cool paper, a condensed grotesque, and no stickers or badges.

Trade-off: Memorable and playful, but the cassette takes up the first screen.

Open mock
E · live colour field

Field

The page background is flat colour bands whose widths are the current mix ratio. Controls sit on opaque white panels. Replaces the purple-glass Aurora.

Trade-off: Ratio is visible everywhere, but the colour is loud.

Open mock
F · plain terminal

Terminal

A monochrome keyboard-first TUI: j/k to move, space to toggle, p to preview, s to save. One status line for real events, no fake logs, no glow.

Trade-off: Fastest for power users, least approachable.

A · hardware

Console

A tactile mixing desk: one channel strip per playlist with a real fader, min/max knobs and a live share meter. Warm grey body, a single orange for actions, LCD readouts, pad-style presets.

Trade-off: the most intuitive controls for ratios, but the chunkiest, tall strips use vertical space.

Open mock
B · pro tool

Studio

A DAW arrangement view. Coloured clip lanes show the generated sequence on a time ruler, a draggable proportion bar sets shares, and a status footer tallies minutes against the target.

Trade-off: the clearest picture of what the mix will actually do, but the densest, it is a power-user screen.

Open mock
C · editorial

Liner Notes

A magazine / record-sleeve layout, no emoji. Numbered chapters, a recipe written as a sentence, a stacked proportion bar, and the preview set as a printed tracklist with dot leaders.

Trade-off: the most distinctive and calm, but a long scroll rather than a one-screen workspace.

Open mock
D · neo-brutalist

Mixtape

Playful bento with thick borders and hard shadows. A cassette is the hero: the mix name is on its label and the tape window re-proportions as you change ratios. Each playlist has its own colour and cover pattern.

Trade-off: the most fun and memorable; borders and shadows eat density, and the style is divisive.

Open mock
E · liquid glass

Aurora

Translucent glass panels over a drifting colour field. The background blobs are the playlists' colours and resize with the priority weights, so the backdrop itself shows the blend ratio.

Trade-off: the most polished and phone-like; legibility over a moving gradient needs care, and blur is GPU-heavy.

Open mock
F · TUI / synth-wave

Terminal

A keyboard-first tmux-style layout with a synth-wave sunset header. j/k to move, space to select, hotkeys for presets, p to preview, s to save. The mouse still works everywhere.

Trade-off: the fastest for power users and the wildest departure, but the least approachable for casual users.
