    Design notes
    Look
    Round 1 hardware, pulled down about a quarter: warm grey body #A3A097, page #6C6860, dark strips #35342F to #282724, pale LCD #BCC09F, one orange #FF6B1A with dark text.
    Type
    Space Grotesk for labels, Space Mono for numbers. 13px minimum.
    Channels
    Round 2 layout in dark panels with Round 1 details: knurled knobs with tick rings, grooved fader caps, an LED share meter, LCD number boxes. The cover tile is small. The share figure is not repeated on the card. It shows in the meter, the master LCD and the preview breakdown.
    Kept from round 4
    Search opens in a dialog. Example order is plain coloured letters. The running-out warning is a plain note.
    Flow
    One page: blend at the top, preview below. Changing a setting marks the preview as out of date until you press Preview.
    Trade-off
    Bevels and grain carry more visual weight than flat panels. They are contained to the hardware, and the data colours stay the loudest thing on screen.
