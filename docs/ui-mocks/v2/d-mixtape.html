Mixtape
Spotify playlist mixer
Mock controls

Mix playlists by ratio.

Pick up to 10 playlists, say how often each one plays, and Mixtape builds one new Spotify playlist in those proportions.

Mixtape asks to read your playlists and create new ones. It never posts, follows, or changes anything you already have.

How it works

  1. Add playlists by searching or pasting a Spotify link.
  2. Set how many songs in a row each one plays and how much weight it gets.
  3. Preview the tracklist, reorder it if you like, then create it on Spotify.

      Design notes

      Palette

      #EEF1F0 paper#FAFBFA label white #12201D ink #FF5A1F orange#0F766E teal #FFB89A orange tint#A9D0CB teal tint #6FAFA8 teal tint 2#1D3A2A overprint

      Fonts

      Barlow Condensed 700 for headings, the mix name and preset names. Barlow 400 to 600 for everything else, numbers included, set with tabular figures. Smallest text is 14px.

      Signature ideas

      1. One cassette, drawn flat in two inks. Its tape window is the meter: the segments re-proportion as ratios change and the two reels wind toward the target.
      2. Each playlist is one ink plus one printed pattern, overprinted with multiply, and that fill follows it through covers, tape, bars and tick boxes.
      3. The preview is a ticket with a dashed tear line. Rows are plain lines; a dragged row gets an outline and nothing else.

      Trade-off

      Two inks cannot give six playlists six distinct hues, so some are tints and overprints and the example squares rely on letters. Calm and consistent, but a little less glanceable than a rainbow.

      Tells I avoided and how

      Flat fills only, one condensed grotesque, no pills, stickers, glow or icon tiles, no hero numbers (totals are one sentence), presets as a list, one level of containers, nothing smaller than 13px, no looping animation.
