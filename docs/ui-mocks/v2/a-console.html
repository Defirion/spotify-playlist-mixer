Playlist Mixer

3/10 playlists

Blend several Spotify playlists into one new playlist

Pick up to 10 of your playlists, choose how much of each one you want and how many songs play in a row, check the running order, then save the result to Spotify.

  1. Add the playlists you want to blend.
  2. Set a priority for each one, and how many songs play together.
  3. Preview the mix, drag tracks into the order you like, and save it.

Saved playlists stay off your profile, but anyone with the link may access them.

      Design notes: Console

      Palette

      #CDD0D2 desk #2E3236 chassis #383D42 strip #F3F5F6 silkscreen #E3E6E8 knob cap #F5F6F6 paper #FF7A1A next step #D95C8A Bachata #2FB49A Salsa #4E8DF0 Kizomba

      Fonts

      Labels, names and buttons use the system sans stack (Segoe UI, then system-ui). IBM Plex Mono is used only for numbers that benefit from fixed-width figures: the priority scale, number fields, track lengths and the example-mix letters. Everything is 13px or larger and in sentence case.

      Signature ideas

      1. Each playlist is a channel strip. Its data colour lives on the fader cap, the meter fill, the cover tile and the example-mix cells, and nowhere else.
      2. The meter beside each fader shows the real share of the mix and changes units between play time and song count with the balance method.
      3. Orange marks the next step only. It is on Preview when the settings have changed, and moves to Create playlist once the preview is current.

      Trade-off

      Tactile control costs height. Each strip is about 560px tall, so with more than four playlists the rack scrolls sideways (and swipes on a phone). A compact list would show ten playlists at once but would lose the fader and meter.

      Tells I avoided and how

      Flat fills and hard 1px edges only, so no colour ramps, glow or blur. System sans plus one mono, no decorative codes or tiny caps labels, no icon tiles, no stat hero, no side stripes, no looping animation, and no em dashes.
