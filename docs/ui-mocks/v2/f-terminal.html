Spotify Playlist Mixer

Mix: Friday Heat

Connect

Spotify Playlist Mixer blends up to 10 of your playlists into one new playlist.

You pick the playlists, say how many songs from each should play in a row and how often, check the running order, then save it to Spotify.

Spotify will ask you to allow two things:

  • read your playlists
  • create playlists for you

Nothing is posted to your profile.

        Design notes

        Palette

        #0C0C0C background #D8D8D8 text #8C8C8C secondary text #555555 borders #E0A100 amber, source 1 #5FB360 green, source 2 #E8553D red-orange, source 3

        Fonts

        • IBM Plex Mono at 14px, weights 400 and 600, for every character
        • Everything sits on one character grid, so columns line up by counting characters

        Signature ideas

        • Off-white on near-black with one highlight: reverse video marks the focused row, and the focused pane gets a double border and an inverted title
        • The blend bar is a row of block characters on the grid. Drag the dividers, or use the arrow keys
        • The status bar shows only the keys that work in the current pane, each one tappable. Toasts replace it for 4 seconds

        Trade-off

        • Speed and density for people who live on the keyboard, at the cost of a first-time visitor needing the hints and the help screen (?) to find things

        Tells avoided and how

        • Flat fills only, one monospace face, no glow or scanlines, no badges, no icon containers, no decorative numbering, no fake status text. Source colour is used only as text colour on the data it belongs to
