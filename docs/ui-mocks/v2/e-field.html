Mix several Spotify playlists into one.

Pick up to ten playlists. Say how much of each you want and how many songs should play in a row. Preview the result, reorder it, then save it to your Spotify account.

  • The colour field behind this page is your mix. Each band is one playlist, as wide as its share.
  • You can balance by song count or by play time.
  • Nothing is saved to Spotify until you press Create.

The app only asks to read your playlists and to create new ones.

Presets

A preset sets the ratios, length and shuffle in one go. You can still change anything after.

    Design notes: E, Field

    Palette

    #E8553D tomato, Bachata Sensual #B5C334 olive, Salsa Romantica (black text on it) #14807B teal, Kizomba Classics (white text on it) #E3A72F mustard, #8C2F1E brick, #4A5A52 slate for the search-only playlists #111 text, rules, and the one primary button #FFFFFF panels, #D6D6D1 inner rules, #EEEEEB idle controls

    Fonts

    Hanken Grotesk, 400 to 700, tabular numerals everywhere so shares and durations do not jitter while you drag. Body 15px, helper text 14px, nothing under 13px. Headings 24 to 40px.

    Signature ideas

    • The screen is the stacked bar. One flat band per playlist, widths exactly equal to each share, re-proportioned over 200ms ease-out (not at all under reduced motion).
    • A 64px ruler at the top repeats the bands with solid white labels and a slim handle at each boundary. Drag it, or type a priority in the panel. On phones it shrinks to a 12px strip.
    • Colour is data only: bands, swatches, the example-mix squares, preset ratio bars, generated flat pattern covers. Everything with text sits on opaque white.

    Trade-off

    On desktop the panels cover most of the field, so the ruler carries the proportions and costs 64px of height. On phones the ruler has no labels or handles, so the share is read from the panel and changed with sliders and fields.

    Tells I avoided and how

    No colour ramps anywhere (flat fills, SVG patterns only), no blur or glass, no glow, no icon containers or pills, presets as a list, one near-black action colour, no decorative motion, sentence-case plain copy.
