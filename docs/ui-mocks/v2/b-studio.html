Design notes

Palette

#F1F2F4 page#FFFFFF panel #14161A ink#7B8189 hairline#D5D8DD inner line #E4572E vermilion#1B998B teal#E0A100 ochre

Source colours are the only saturated colours on screen. They appear on timeline blocks, the share bar, swatches, cover tiles and the summary bar, and nowhere else. Ink (near black) is the only other emphasis: primary button and current selection.

Fonts

IBM Plex Sans for all text. IBM Plex Mono only for times and numbers, so columns of durations line up. Nothing is smaller than 13px.

Signature ideas

  • Lanes on a ruler. One row per playlist, flat solid blocks per run. Drag a block edge to change the minimum or maximum songs in a row.
  • Hatched tail. Where a playlist runs out of songs its lane turns into diagonal hatching, with one sentence and two text buttons under the timeline.
  • One bar for the shares. A single draggable proportion bar with numeric fields in the table below it, plus a bottom summary bar that says the totals in plain words.

Trade-off

Density and a drafting-table look over friendliness. Every action is a word, not an icon, so rows are wider. On a phone the timeline scrolls sideways instead of shrinking, and the fixed summary bar takes about 90px of height.

Tells I avoided and how

No blends or glows (flat fills, hard 1px lines, one hard 3px offset on menus and toasts), no pills or badges, no icon containers, no card side stripes, no hero numbers (totals are sentences), no small type, no fake status text, no decorative animation, no em dashes. Light cool-grey ground with a named typeface instead of dark mode with an indigo accent or beige with a serif.

PrivacyTerms

Mix several playlists into one, in proportions you choose

Pick up to ten of your Spotify playlists. Say how many songs from each should play in a row and how much of the mix each one gets. The app lays the whole set out on a timeline so you can see it before anything is saved.

  • Add playlists, or start from a preset.
  • Set the runs and the shares, by song count or by play time.
  • Check the timeline, reorder tracks, then save the mix to Spotify.
The app reads your playlists and creates new ones. It changes nothing else.
