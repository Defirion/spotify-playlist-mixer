The Mixer

An occasional papera weighted playlist blender

An invitation

Several playlists, one evening, blended by the hour.

The Mixer takes the playlists you already love and folds them into a single, well-paced list. You choose how much of each, and how many songs in a row, and it does the arithmetic so the night keeps its shape.

  1. 01
    Choose your sources.

    Search your library or paste a Spotify link. Up to ten playlists per mix.

  2. 02
    Write the recipe.

    Drag a divider to set each playlist’s share, by play time or by song count.

  3. 03
    Print the tracklist.

    Preview, reorder by hand, then save it to Spotify in a single press.

We ask only to read your playlists and to create new ones. Nothing is posted to your profile.

    The mix so far

    Most playlists are made by hand and finished by accident. This one is made by recipe: a few sources, a proportion for each, a length, and a little shuffle. Read down the page as you would a short feature — five chapters, each one a decision — and by the end you will hold a tracklist you can still edit with your own hands.

          Colophon & design notes direction C · liner notes

          Palette

          #F5F0E8 paper #1A1A1A ink #C8552B rust #2F6B6A teal #9A6E16 ochre #6B3D5E plum

          One accent only. The three muted inks are reserved for sources and appear as swatches, rules and small letters.

          Type

          Fraunces (variable: optical size, SOFT, WONK) for display, numerals and italic moments; Newsreader for text; Inter only for the mock controls. Tabular lining figures for every number; true small caps for labels; balanced and pretty text-wrap; hanging opening quotes on the pull-quote; a 62-character measure.

          Trade-off

          Atmosphere over density. The long single column reads beautifully and teaches the order of decisions, but it asks for more scrolling than a dashboard would, and the fine controls live in the margin where they are easy to miss on a phone.

          Three signature ideas

          1. The recipe as a sentence. The balance method, length and per-source runs are written as prose with inline, editable words — the sentence rewrites itself as you move anything.
          2. A proportion bar like a printed infographic. Dividers set each share; a thin lower bar shows what the last preview actually delivered, so target and actual sit one above the other, with a numeric ledger as fallback.
          3. Magazine apparatus for a tool. Roman chapter numerals, a running table of contents with progress and a live summary in the margin, an Editor’s note for the exhaustion warning, a pull-quote for the stats and a footnote for the privacy line.
